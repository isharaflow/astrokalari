About Sreevalsan

A closer look at the guru lineage, ancestral heritage and traditional arts that continue to shape my path in Jyothisha.

My Guru

From Guru to Tradition

My journey in Jyothisha began when I was just seven years old.

From my father's initial guidance to further traditional studies at Padumanna, my path has been shaped by generations of knowledge, Sanskrit learning and the traditional methods of Jyothisha and Prashna.

Today, after 16 years of dedicated learning and practice, I continue to walk the path of traditional Jyothisha with humility and respect.

Ancestral HomeMadassery Kalari, Malappuram
Madassery Kalarickal

The Heritage of Madassery Kalari

Jyothisha is not simply a profession for me. It is part of a family and cultural heritage.

My great-grandfather was both a Kalari Ashan and astrologer. Our ancestral connection with these traditional disciplines continues through our family home, known as Madassery Kalari.

This heritage has shaped my understanding of discipline, learning, tradition and service.

Shared HeritageJyothisha, Kalari, Sanskrit & Music
Music & Traditional Arts

A Life Connected to Tradition

My connection with traditional culture also extends to Carnatic music and flute.

I have studied Carnatic music under Poonthanam Unnikrishnan Sir and Angadippuram Sreedevi Teacher, and learned flute under Tirur Mohanan Sir.

For me, Jyothisha, Kalari, Sanskrit and music represent different expressions of a shared cultural heritage.

Traditional Knowledge for the Present

Ancient Wisdom. Meaningful Guidance.

The ancient traditions of Jyothisha were developed through generations of observation, study and experience.

My approach is to preserve these traditional foundations while communicating them in a clear and understandable manner.

Every person's circumstances are different. Each consultation is therefore approached with individual attention, traditional principles and sincere discussion.
