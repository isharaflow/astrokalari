Introduction

A Journey Rooted in Tradition

Mr.Sreevalsan is a practitioner of Traditional Vedic Astrology in kerala with 16 years of proffessional experience in the study and practice of kerala classical traditional astrology.

His journey began at the age of seven under the guidance of his father, Rajan Panicker, who became his first Guru and introduced him to the foundational principles of astrology. After graduating in commerce, his pursuit of traditional knowledge led him to Padumanna Kalari, where he had the privilege of studying under the renowned astrologer Gopalakrishna Panicker. Under his guidance, he pursued the study of Sanskrit and traditional astrology and its applications, including Ashtamangala Prashna and Thaboola Prashna (Extreme classical level of vedic Kerala Astrology) texts with practical lesson.

Today, Sreevalsan continues to uphold this Guru–Shishya tradition while bringing a thoughtful and contemporary approach to astrological consultation.

Frequently asked questions

Astrology: Ancient Wisdom for the Modern World Astrology is a profound traditional science with a long and evolving history. Ancient India made significant contributions to the development of astrological knowledge through the wisdom and observations of its revered sages and Rishis.

The ancient Indian Rishis developed numerous astrological principles to understand and anticipate challenges—not only in human life, but also in relation to the broader patterns and circumstances affecting living beings. These principles have been preserved and explained through classical Sanskrit texts such as Prashna Marga, Hora Shastra, Saravali, and other traditional Granthas.
Astrologers can be regarded as interpreters of this ancient knowledge. Their role is to connect time-tested astrological principles with the circumstances of modern life and offer meaningful guidance for the challenges people face today. In this sense, astrology is not merely about prediction; it is an art of interpretation, observation, and thoughtful application.
At the same time, astrological guidance should not be understood as a guaranteed solution to every situation. The effectiveness of any traditional remedy may depend upon many factors, including an individual's actions, circumstances, discipline, and spiritual awareness. Just as the effectiveness of certain medicines can be influenced by lifestyle and daily habits, traditional astrological remedies are best approached with understanding, consistency, and personal responsibility.
Spirituality, in this context, is not limited to worship or religious practice. It can also be understood as a path toward inner stillness, clarity, and a calmer mind—creating the awareness needed to make the right decision and take the next step in life.
When a person feels uncertain, overwhelmed, or temporarily stuck, the wisdom of traditional astrology can serve as a means of reflection and guidance. It may help one understand the circumstances, regain clarity, and approach the next move in life with greater awareness and confidence.
