About Us

Preserving the Timeless Heritage of Kerala Vedic Astrology

Sri Sreevalsan Panicker is a traditional Kerala Vedic Astrologer with over 16 years of dedicated experience, committed to serving individuals and families through the ancient wisdom of Vedic astrology, guided by faith, discipline and Kerala's sacred traditions.

His introduction to astrology began at home under his father, his first Guru, and deepened through formal traditional learning under the respected Guru Gopalakrishnan. Alongside astrology, he is a dedicated Kalaripayattu practitioner, trained in a traditional Kalari maintained by his family — an art that instils discipline, balance, focus and harmony between body and mind.

Holding a Bachelor's degree in Commerce, he combines disciplined, analytical thinking with traditional knowledge, guiding clients on marriage, career, education, business, family, finances, health and spiritual growth — with complete confidentiality, respect and personal attention.
